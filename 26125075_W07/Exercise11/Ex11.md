A. 10, 2, 4
B. 20, 4, 4
C. 16, 2, 2
D. 20, 2, 2
-------------------------
Answer: B. 20, 4, 4
As every single integer has 4 bytes, in the array we have 5 integers. Thus, sizeof(arr) return the size of the array in bytes, whichs is 4*5 = 20 bytes.
Similary, *arr is equivalent to arr[0] as the pointer pointing to the first element. Then, the result would be 4 bytes (size of an integer).
