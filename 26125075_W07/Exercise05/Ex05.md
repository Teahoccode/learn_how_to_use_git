Given the following code:
1. int *p , *q , v , nom[5];
2. p = &v;
3. *p = 12;
4. q = p;
5. nom[0] = *q;
6. p = nom;
7. p++;
8. nom[2] = 12;
9. *p = 13;
10. *q = 10;
11. v = 11;
12. *(p+3) = 16;
13. p = &nom[3];
14. *p = 10;
15. p--;

What are the last values of *p , *q , v and nom ?
------------------------------

Answer:

The last value of *p is: 12
Explain: 
First, p = &v --> *p = 12 --> p = nom (p points to nom[0]) --> p++ (p points to nom[1]) --> *p = 13 (nom[1] = 13) --> p = &nom[3] --> *p = 10 --> p-- (p points to nom[2], where nom[2] = 12), thus *p = 12.

The last value of *q is: 11
Explain:
In line 4: We have  p = &v [line2] and *p = 12 --> then q = p (*q = 12, q points to v) --> *q = 10 [line 10] --> v = 11 (thus *q = 11) 

The last value of v is: 11
Explain:
As I presented before, in line 11, v = 11 and only q points to v. After that, there is no alteration to the value of v or *q. Thus, the last value of v is 11.

The last value of nom is: [12, 13, 12, 10, 16]
Explain:
nom[0] = 12 [line 5] --> nom[2] = 12 [line 8] --> nom[1] = 13 [line 9, after p = nom and p++] --> nom[4] = 16 [line 12, via *(p+3)] --> nom[3] = 10 [line 14, after p = &nom[3]]
